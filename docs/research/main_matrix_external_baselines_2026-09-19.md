## Reading

- The coordinate-system effect still dominates every dataset.
- Among label-free methods, the simplest classical reliability weighting
  (inverse local jitter per view and joint) is the best row on both real
  datasets and is statistically better than A6 on the private held-out set.
  It matches A6 on Unity. This is a substantive finding for the paper: the
  learned model does not beat a well-chosen classical fusion rule.
- Kalman RTS smoothing is the second-best row on FreeMan; on the private data
  temporal smoothing (RTS, Butterworth, output smoothing) slightly hurts,
  consistent with the paper's remark that smoothing attenuates the fast
  trunk rotation that the pseudo-reference retains.
- A transferred generic refiner (SmoothNet, Human3.6M weights) is worse than
  averaging on every dataset, so "any pretrained refiner" is not a substitute.
- Unity remains dominated by the shared monocular scale/depth error (all
  post-estimation rows 174–190 mm versus 30 mm for calibrated triangulation).

## B1 diagnosis (why the plain TCN is so poor)

B1 reaches a *higher* self-supervised validation score than A6 (0.879 vs
0.766) yet 147 mm on the held-out people. Inspection of person 1 shows the
unbounded residual shrinks the skeleton non-uniformly (hip-shoulder distance
0.538 m in the base pose, 0.313 m in the B1 output; mean residual 16 cm), which
lowers the scale-dependent acceleration objective from 1458 to 333 while the
bone-length term (weight 1, small magnitude) barely resists. The self-
supervised objective set is therefore only well-posed together with the
bounded residual; an unconstrained network exploits it. B2 (same network,
A6's 5 cm tanh bound) separates the architecture effect from the bound: it
trains normally (no saturation, mean per-axis residual 1.1 cm), lands at
63.30 mm on the private held-out set (+2.5 mm vs A6, the same level as A4),
103.17 mm PA on FreeMan (indistinguishable from A6 and the deterministic
cluster) and 178.33 mm on Unity (same as A6). So the residual bound, not the
rotation-aware architecture, is what keeps the self-supervised objective
well-posed; the architecture itself buys about 2.5 mm on the private data and
nothing on FreeMan or Unity. Both rows should be reported.

## A6 residual saturation (paper-critical)

The paper's A6 checkpoint (`all137_a6_e100_seed0`) has a degenerate residual:
in the canonical frame every valid joint of every frame of all 928 cycles is
displaced by exactly (-0.05, -0.05, -0.05) m, i.e. all three tanh outputs sit
at -1. The learned correction is a constant translation, which the
per-cycle similarity alignment and hip centring remove, so A6 equals the
quality-weighted mean A3 up to that offset. Checked over all inference
outputs (`kpts_fused_canonical - kpts_base_canonical`):

| Run | Fraction of points with all axes at the bound | Mean per-axis residual |
|---|---:|---:|
| A4 seed 0 | 0.000 | 1.1 cm |
| A5 seed 0 | 0.000 | 0.95 cm |
| **A6 seed 0 (paper)** | **1.000** | **5.0 cm** |
| A6 seed 1 | 0.000 | 0.54 cm |
| A6 seed 2 | 0.000 | 0.48 cm |
| A7 seed 0 | 0.000 | 0.49 cm |
| cohort OOF folds 1, 2, 5, 7, 8 | 1.000 | 5.0 cm |
| cohort OOF folds 3, 4, 6, 9 | 0.000 | 0.3–0.7 cm |
| B1 (unbounded) | 0.297 (of 5 cm) | 12.6 cm |

So the collapse is a seed/fold-specific optimum of the A6 objective (it
appears only with the complete-cycle ROM term active: A5 never saturates,
A6 does in 6 of 12 trainings), not a property of the architecture. It
affects: Table 1 row A6 and all A6-derived rows (corruption diagnostics,
FreeMan/Unity zero-shot A6 seed 0), and 5 of the 9 cohort OOF folds in the
case study. Recommended handling: report A6 over the three seeds (or use a
non-degenerate seed as the headline), add the saturation fraction as a
declared diagnostic, and re-run the cohort OOF folds that collapsed (or note
which folds contributed constant-offset predictions; descriptors are
pelvis-relative so the offset is largely removed, but this must be verified).

## FreeMan in-domain training (subject-disjoint, five folds, 12 epochs)

Out-of-fold A6 trained on FreeMan itself: PA 103.21 mm, Sim3 364.32 mm.
Paired over the ten subjects: -0.16 mm PA versus the zero-shot seed-0 A6
[-0.28, -0.06], 9/10 subjects, p_Holm 0.039; +0.02 mm versus the body-frame
average [-0.06, +0.10], p_Holm 0.43. Training in the target domain therefore
recovers the seed-0 offset artefact but does not beat deterministic
averaging, and the reliability-weighted average (102.31) stays ahead. None of
the five fold checkpoints shows residual saturation. Artefacts:
`local/runs/freeman_trained_fusion/report/`.
